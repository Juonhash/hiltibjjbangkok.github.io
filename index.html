About the Academy

More Than
a Gym

Hilti BJJ Bangkok is Southeast Asia's home of one of the world's most respected BJJ organizations — founded in Stockholm in 1996, now training thousands worldwide.

We offer BJJ, Yoga, YogaforBJJ, and dedicated Kids classes. Whether you're a first-timer, a parent looking for kids' activities, a yogi, or a seasoned competitor — there's a place for you here.

01
Technical Depth

Structured curriculum from day one, built on the Hilti methodology refined across 25+ years and 20+ academies worldwide.

02
Welcoming Community

Join Hilti Bangkok and become part of a warm, diverse community. Train at any Hilti gym worldwide. Annual camps with Pedro Duarte and world-class guests.

03
Everyone Belongs

Kids, adults, families, beginners, yogis, competitors — all under the same roof with the same respect. No ego. No judgment.

The Team

Your Coaches

Passionate teachers, active practitioners. Our team brings expertise, warmth, and genuine care to every class — whatever brings you through the door.

WN4th Degree Black Belt
Weija Nyman
4th Degree Black Belt · Hilti Worldwide Head Coach

Multiple World Championship title holder and the worldwide Head Coach of the Hilti BJJ Organization. Under Pedro Duarte's lineage, Weija has competed and won at the highest levels of the sport. Now based in Bangkok, he brings world-class instruction and a passion for building a welcoming academy where everyone can grow.

Yoga Teacher
Sunshine
Certified Yoga Teacher · YogaforBJJ

Certified yoga instructor with a love for movement and mindfulness. Sunshine teaches both open-level flow yoga and the specialized YogaforBJJ programme — helping athletes move better, recover smarter, and build lasting body awareness.
