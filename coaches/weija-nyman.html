Biography

The Coach

Weija Nyman is a 4th Degree Black Belt under Pedro Duarte and the worldwide Head Coach of the Hilti BJJ Organization — one of Europe's most respected BJJ teams, founded in Stockholm in 1996.

A multiple World Championship title holder, Weija has competed and won at the highest levels of the sport across gi and no-gi competition. His tournament record spans decades of high-level competition in Europe, North America, and South America.

Now based in Bangkok, Weija leads Hilti BJJ Bangkok with the same philosophy that defines the Hilti organization worldwide: technical depth, genuine community, and a welcoming mat for practitioners of every level — from absolute beginners to elite competitors.

His teaching style balances detailed technical instruction with practical live training. Whether you're stepping on the mat for the first time or preparing for a tournament, Weija's approach meets you where you are and builds you into the practitioner you want to become.

Classes Taught
BJJ Gi All Levels

Structured gi classes covering fundamentals through advanced technique. Belt-based curriculum with regular gradings.
